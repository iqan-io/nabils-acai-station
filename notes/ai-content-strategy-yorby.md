# AI-Generated Social Content Strategy (Yorby case study)

Source: YouTube walkthrough of how the creator scaled their app "Yorby" to
$5,000/month in revenue using 100% AI-generated social content — no real-life
filming. Saved for reference when planning future content workflows.

## Overview (0:00-0:30)
End-to-end workflow for producing social content with AI generation instead
of filming.

## 1. Account Warm-up (1:52-2:51)
- Before posting, "warm up" new social accounts for 3-5 days.
- Behave like a real user: scroll, like, comment.
- Goal: avoid bot detection.

## 2. Content Strategy (3:00-4:38)
- Favored format: "hook and demo" — an AI-generated person delivers a
  compelling hook, followed by a product demo.
- Non-talking AI content currently performs better than talking-head AI
  content, due to public sentiment/skepticism toward AI avatars (3:07-4:00).

## 3. Remixing Viral Content (6:46-8:37)
- Don't start from scratch — find successful videos online and remix them
  for your own product.

## 4. Generation Pipeline ("the sauce")
1. **First frame** (8:37-10:24): Nano Banana — generate a still
   avatar/face-swap image.
2. **Hook video** (10:24-12:27): Kling 3.0 — animate the first frame into a
   short video clip from a prompt.
3. **Editing** (12:27-14:10): Claude Sonnet for script remixing; FFmpeg to
   stitch hook + demo + text overlays together.

## Wrap-up (14:33-16:20)
- Creator's own tool, **Social Q**, automates this whole pipeline.
- Alternative: "vibe code" a custom version using the same models
  (Nano Banana, Kling 3.0, Claude Sonnet, FFmpeg).
